The cut comes before the style.

Every appointment starts with a conversation: what your hair does now, what you want it to do on a Tuesday, and how much time you actually have in the morning. The cut follows that answer, not a photograph.

Fine hair gets weight removed, not layered into oblivion. Curls get cut dry, section by section, so the shape survives wash day. Grey gets blended to grow out softly.

  • Consultation firstBefore any big change, the shape and the upkeep get talked through.
  • Cut for your routineIf you won’t style it at home, it shouldn’t be cut that way.
  • Notes for next timeSo the second visit starts where the first one finished.

Before you book

The questions asked most often at the desk. If yours is not here, get in touch and ask; the salon would rather talk it through.

How do I book?

Contact the salon directly. That is the quickest way to check availability and find a time that suits.

Do you take walk-ins?

Availability varies. Booking ahead is the safest way to get the chair and the time you want.

What happens at the first visit?

Expect a conversation about your hair before anything starts: what it does now, what you want, and how much upkeep you can live with.

I am not sure what I want yet.

Ask for a consultation. It is the easiest way to land on the right cut, colour or treatment before committing.

What is the cancellation policy?

Ask when you book. The salon confirms its own policy, including any deposit or notice period.
