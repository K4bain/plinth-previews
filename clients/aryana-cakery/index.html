The useful bits, written plainly.

How do I book?

Contact the salon directly. That is the quickest way to check the diary and find a time.

Can I walk in for cupcakes and pastries?

Yes — visit the shop on Stanningley Road for counter cupcakes, slices and sweet boxes.

Can I bring a photo?

Yes — bring it to talk about shape, length or colour rather than to copy another head exactly.

What about cancellations?

Ask when you book. The salon confirms its own notice period and any deposit.
