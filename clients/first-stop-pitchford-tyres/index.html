Ask us

The things people ask at the desk. If yours is not here, get in touch.

How do I check if my tyre size is in stock?

Read the size off your tyre sidewall (for example 205/55 R16) and call 01924 265344.

Can you repair a punctured tyre?

We inspect the tyre internally first. If the puncture is in the safe repair zone and the sidewall is undamaged, we can repair and rebalance it.

Do you fit van and 4x4 tyres?

Yes — we supply and fit car, light commercial van and SUV/4x4 tyres across budget and branded ranges.
