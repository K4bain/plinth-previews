Before
you book.

The practical notes, kept short.

How do I book my bike in?

Call +44 7791 994709 or email with your motorcycle make, model and the work needed to check bench availability.

What types of motorcycles do you work on?

Road bikes, off-road/enduro machines and competition/trials motorcycles.

Do you supply tyres, chains and service parts?

Yes — we supply and fit tyres, brake pads, chain & sprocket kits, oils, filters and OEM or quality aftermarket parts.

Where on Healey Road are you based?

Healey Road, Ossett WF5 8LN. Always call ahead before bringing a bike down on a van or trailer.
