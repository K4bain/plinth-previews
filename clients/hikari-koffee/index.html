The idea

A good cup is a small, precise thing. Clean extraction, whisked matcha and a room that lets you slow down.

Every shot and pour-over is weighed and dialled in daily, alongside ceremonial-grade matcha, hojicha and a short counter display of fresh bakes.

Before
you visit.

The practical notes, kept short.

Do I need to book a table?

No booking needed — Hikari is walk-in for both sit-in tables and takeaway coffee or matcha.

Do you offer oat or alternative milk?

Yes. Oat and dairy milk are available across espresso drinks, iced lattes and whisked matcha.

Where in Didsbury are you located?

2 Albert Hill Street, Didsbury, Manchester M20 6RF — tucked just off Wilmslow Road.

Can I get everything to take away?

Yes. All hot and iced coffees, matcha drinks and counter bakes are available to take out.
