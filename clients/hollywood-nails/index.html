Before
you book.

The practical bits, without the small print.

How do I book?

Contact the salon directly. That is the quickest way to check the diary and confirm a time.

Do you take walk-ins?

When the diary allows. Booking ahead is the safest way to get the chair and time you want.

Can I bring a reference photo?

Yes — bring your nail inspiration photo to show the shape, length, shade or nail art you have in mind.

What is the cancellation policy?

Ask when you book. The salon confirms its own notice period and any deposit.
