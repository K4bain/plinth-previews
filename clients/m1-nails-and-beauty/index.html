Questions,
answered.

The useful details, without a wall of policy text.

How do I book?

Contact the salon directly. That is the fastest way to check the diary and confirm a time.

What if I have gel or acrylics on already?

Let us know when you book or arrive so we can allow time for a safe soak-off or infill.

Can I bring a nail design photo?

Yes. Show us any shade, French tip or nail art reference at the start of your appointment.

What if I need to cancel?

Ask when you book. The salon confirms its own notice period and any deposit.
