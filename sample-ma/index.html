The idea

A good haircut is a small, precise thing. It should make the rest of the day feel lighter.

We take the time to look at how the hair falls, how you wear it and how much time you actually have in the morning. Then we cut for that life, not for the mirror.

Before
you book.

The practical notes, kept short.

How do I book?

Contact the salon directly. That is the quickest way to check the diary and confirm a time.

Do you take walk-ins?

When the diary allows. Booking ahead is the safest way to get the chair and time you want.

Can I bring a reference photo?

Yes — bring it to talk about shape, length or colour rather than to copy another head exactly.

What is the cancellation policy?

Ask when you book. The salon confirms its own notice period and any deposit.
