The room,
in low light.

No queue, no performance. A working salon with good tools, warm water and enough time to finish properly.

Sharp, not severe.

The line should be clean without looking punishing. We cut for movement as well as shape: enough weight where the hair needs it, enough removal where it does not.

Colour is treated the same way. A precise tone, a clean fade and a plan for the next appointment — not a shade that only works under salon lighting.

Before
you book.

The practical notes, kept short.

How do I book?

Contact the salon directly. That is the quickest way to check the diary and confirm a time.

Do you take walk-ins?

When the diary allows. Booking ahead is the safest way to get the chair and time you want.

Can I bring a reference photo?

Yes — bring it to talk about shape, length or colour rather than to copy another head exactly.

What is the cancellation policy?

Ask when you book. The salon confirms its own notice period and any deposit.
