Good to know

How do I book?

Get in touch by phone or email: that's the quickest way to check availability and find a time that suits.

Can I just walk in?

Booking ahead is the safest way to get the time you want — get in touch to check what's free today.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Arrive with dry hair unless you're told otherwise, and bring any inspiration photos for a colour or a restyle.

A quick chat before your appointment (or at the start of it) is the easiest way to land on the right cut, colour or treatment.
