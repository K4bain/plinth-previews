Plan your visit

Precision, without the performance.

A good appointment feels like a conversation, not a reveal. We look at how the hair falls, where it holds volume, and what your mornings actually allow. Then we cut for that.

Colour is planned in layers: the tone now, the fade later, the next appointment before it is needed. Nothing is done just for the before-and-after photo.

Questions,
answered.

The useful details, without a wall of policy text.

How do I book?

Contact the salon directly. That is the fastest way to check the diary and confirm a time.

Should I arrive with clean hair?

Come as you are. The salon will wash and prep the hair as part of the service where needed.

Can I bring a reference photo?

Yes. Bring it to talk about shape, length or colour — not to copy someone else's head exactly.

What if I need to cancel?

Ask when you book. The salon confirms its own notice period and any deposit.
